## Full description (max 4000)

English:
```
Khmer Dubber turns a video and its subtitle file (.srt) into a video with a natural Khmer voice, automatically, right on your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose a video (MP4, MKV, MOV…)
2. Choose its subtitle file (.srt), in Chinese, English, Thai, Korean or any other language
3. Tap Start. The subtitles are translated into Khmer, read aloud with a Khmer voice, and mixed into the video.

FEATURES
• Two natural Khmer voices: Sreymom (female) and Piseth (male)
• Start a line with [M] or [F] to give different characters different voices
• The voice follows each subtitle's timing; long lines are sped up just enough to fit
• The original sound stays quietly in the background and is lowered while the Khmer voice speaks
• No subtitle file? Choose only the video and the app writes the subtitle from its sound
• Dub many episodes at once: ep01.mp4 + ep01.srt, ep02.mp4 + ep02.srt…
• Put your logo and lower third on every video: a picture, Khmer text, or an animated green-screen / blue-screen video (MP4, MOV)
• Separate layouts for landscape (16:9) and vertical (9:16) videos
• Keeps working in the background with a progress notification
• A step-by-step guide in Khmer for first-time users
• Khmer subtitles are saved too (.srt)

Videos are saved to Movies/KhmerDubber and subtitles to Download/KhmerDubber.

An internet connection is needed for translation and for the Khmer voice. Only the subtitle text is sent; your videos stay on your phone.

Please only dub videos that you have the right to use.
```
